PC Block "18+" list

The "18+" site list embedded in PC Block is built from the "adult" category of the UT1
blacklists (Université Toulouse 1 Capitole, author Fabrice Prigent):
  https://dsi.ut-capitole.fr/blacklists/
Taken from the mirror https://github.com/olbat/ut1-blacklists at a pinned commit
(file tools/adultlist/ut1.commit in the PC Block source code):
  03501c64d230df9d84124176558d758351027be1

License of the UT1 blacklists: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(CC BY-SA 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: domains were normalized (lower case, no "www." prefix, IDN as punycode) and stored
as 44-bit FNV-1a hashes in the file adult.bin. The file adult.bin is distributed under the
same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.
